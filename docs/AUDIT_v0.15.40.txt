Scope

Catch-resilience checks
- Enemy catch pressure -> bounded pick response bonus.
- Our catch pressure -> bounded ban response bonus.
- Direct self-save coefficient 1.35 > ally-save coefficient 0.72.
- Activation baseline: catch 7.
- Maximum response bonus: +7.5.
- Low catch pressure produces no forced bonus.
- Score detail explicitly exposes the new catch-survival factor.
- Completed comp metrics expose both Catch and Survival.
- ROLE Grade impact: false.
- Riot Grade impact: false.

Draft-order investigation
- Standard draft step sequence was reviewed and was already correct.
- The defect was visual side placement: local views stayed Our-left/Enemy-right, and online mock draft stayed Team-A-left/Team-B-right even when the opposite side was BLUE.
- v0.15.40 keeps the draft sequence unchanged and normalizes the visible board to BLUE-left / RED-right.

Limitations
