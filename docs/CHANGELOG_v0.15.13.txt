2) ROLE GRADE v3 balance

3) Sustain split
- Ally sustain now uses explicit teammate-heal + teammate-shield fields.
- Self sustain uses totalHeal separately.
- Prevents self-healing champions from accidentally receiving support-grade sustain credit and avoids combining ambiguous heal fields into one inflated value.
