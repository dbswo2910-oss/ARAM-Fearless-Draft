Design safeguards
- No role-wide death immunity.
- No champion fixed +10 style bonus.
- No refund without timeline evidence.
- No refund when execution evidence <50.
- No double reward of CC/engage/absorb: refund is bounded by counterfactual survival-caused raw-score loss.
- Exact spell-hit/replay-scene claims are not made.
